## 4 — German word-card content: Fail

Real German Wiktionary API responses and the current parser/card renderer were
used for `ohne`, `mit`, `helfen`, `gehen`, `versprochen`, `was`, plus case samples
and an intentionally nonexistent word.

Passes:

- The monitored uncached `Baum` lookup requested only `de.wiktionary.org`.
  Active dictionary records and source links were German-only.
- `ohne` has a source meaning and examples; `was` keeps separate numbered
  meanings; the nonexistent entry did not fabricate meanings/examples.
- Explicit form lookup for `versprochen` resolved `versprechen`.

Failures:

1. **gehen has no parsed definitions**, despite source meanings being present.
   The actual card displays `Keine Wörterbuchdefinition verfügbar.`
2. **mit combines different source sections.** The parser uses adverb meanings
   such as `wie andere, zusammen mit anderen`, while associating preposition
   examples such as `Ich gehe mit Freunden spazieren.` using repeated numeric
   sense IDs. The actual preposition source meaning is `etwas oder jemandem
   angeschlossen ...`; its global `mit Dativ` annotation is missed. Numeric
   sense matching alone is insufficient when entry sections differ.
3. **Role/usage annotations remain in definition content**, e.g.
   `Interrogativpronomen`, `Indefinitpronomen`, `Relativpronomen` and
   `umgangssprachlich` for `was`.
4. **Already quoted examples get quoted again**, producing `„„...““`.

Relevant artifacts: `live-dictionary.json`, `dictionary-source-evidence.json`,
`live-card-render.json`, `mit-section-mismatch.json`, `missing-entry.json`,
`dictionary-provenance.json`, `dictionary-storage-provenance.json`.

Passes:

- `ohne` correctly separates `mit Akkusativ` into Grammar.
- `versprochen` resolves the infinitive `versprechen` and keeps past/participle/
  perfect forms. `helfen` and `gehen` also expose several source-backed forms.
- Synthetic simple `mit Dativ oder Akkusativ` and `mit Genitiv` annotations
  preserve their wording/operator. Ordinary prose mentioning Dativ is not
  stripped by the narrow helper.

Failures:

1. For **helfen**, source `(mit Dativ, seltener mit Akkusativ)` becomes Grammar
   `mit Dativ`, leaving `seltener mit Akkusativ)` at the start of the meaning.
   The rare-case qualification is not properly preserved/separated.
2. **Present-tense conjugations are absent**, despite real source tables showing
   e.g. `ich helfe`, `du hilfst`, `er/sie/es hilft`.
3. **wegen** leaves `Postposition mit Genitiv` in its definition, with no
   government rules in Grammar.
4. **auf** leaves `Wechselpräposition, mit Dativ/Akkusativ` and related
   location/direction conditions in its definitions, with no separated rules.
5. **trotz** leaves its Genitiv/Dativ annotation and qualification in the
   definition, with no separated rules.
